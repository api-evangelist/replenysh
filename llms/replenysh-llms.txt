# Replenysh

> Replenysh runs your circular supply chain. It routes, recovers, verifies, and optimizes material flows across a 5,000+ partner recovery network, giving enterprises one control layer for reverse logistics.

## Platform
- [Platform overview](https://replenysh.com/): How Replenysh runs circular material flows end to end — routing, verification, chain of custody, and settlement.
- [Recovery network](https://replenysh.com/network): For recovery facilities and haulers. Get matched to loads that fit your facility and get paid faster across 5,000+ partners.
- [Contact](https://replenysh.com/contact): Book a demo or join the recovery network.
